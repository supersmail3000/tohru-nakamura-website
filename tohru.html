Availability

Reservations are released by us for a certain period during the year. Approximately four months in advance.

Due to varying table capacities, it is possible that tables for two people are no longer available, but tables for four people can still be booked. We thank you for your flexibility and ask for your understanding that our general conditions do not allow for any other arrangement.

Please indicate any food intolerances or allergies when making your reservation.

If you cancel less than 48 hours prior to your reserved date, we reserve the right to charge a cancellation fee of 450€ per person. This also applies to no-shows despite a confirmed reservation.

Accessibility

Origin

The building at Burgstraße 5 dates back to the 14th century — rebuilt in stone after Munich's great fire of 1327, acquired by the city in 1550. Step through the red wooden door and you feel every one of those 700 years.

Chef Tohru Nakamura's cooking is shaped by his German-Japanese heritage. Trained in Europe's finest kitchens and in Japan, he brings together European flavours and the philosophy of kaiseki — with meticulous attention to local, seasonal ingredients.

The result is neither strictly Japanese nor German, but something entirely its own — a cuisine built on precision, curiosity and respect for what each season offers.
